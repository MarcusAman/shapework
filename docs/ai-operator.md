# shapework. AI Operator Specification

## Overview
The **AI Operator** is not a generic chatbot. It is a highly tailored workspace that integrates rich contextual information with structured actions, decision-making aids, and conversational capability.

---

## Workspace Structure

### 1. Persistent Operator Dock
Located at the bottom of the sidebar (or as a persistent console), the Operator Dock supports:
* **Text Input**: Direct command prompt.
* **Voice / Audio note**: Capture verbal instructions from a busy operator.
* **File / Image Upload**: Attach contracts, screenshots, or emails.
* **Contextual Prompts**: Suggested commands that update dynamically based on the active screen.

### 2. Conversational Workspace
Instead of returning walls of text, the AI Operator returns interactive, structured UI elements:
* **Executive Summaries**: High-level bullet points explaining deal status or capacity blocks.
* **Ranked Tables & Queues**: Highlighted transaction risks or overdue actions.
* **Approval Cards**: Prepared drafts, reminders, or scheduling proposals waiting for human validation.
* **Interactive Charts**: Clear visualizations of response time and compliance trends.

---

## Response Guidelines
Every answer from the AI Operator must contain:
1. **Direct Conclusion**: A plain, human-friendly summary.
2. **Supporting Evidence**: Sourced emails, dates, or documents explaining the reasoning.
3. **Recommended Next Actions**: What should be done to resolve the risk.
4. **Validation and Execution Controls**: One-click approval, modification, or delegation.
