# First Brokerage Pilot Onboarding Walkthrough

**Audience**: Brokerage Owner, Operations Lead, Transaction Coordinator (TC), Marketing Coordinator  
**Duration**: 25 Minutes  

---

## 1. Welcome to shapework. (2 Mins)
### What it is
* shapework. is the **operating layer** for running your brokerage.
* It coordinates tasks across your team and connected software tools.
* It tracks active transactions, flags compliance bottlenecks, drafts agent reminders, and organizes marketing files in one clear list.

### What it is not
* It is **not** a CRM or transaction system replacement.
* It is **not** a generic chatbot or simple messaging widget.
* It does not replace your human expertise; it acts as a shield, organizing details so you can focus on clients.

---

## 2. Sitting Above Your Infrastructure (3 Mins)
* Your existing tools (Rechat, Dotloop, email, and files) are your connection layer.
* shapework. sits **above** these tools. It extracts key files, reviews dates, and checks for missing information.
* Instead of logging into multiple systems and checking lists manually, shapework. brings active task gaps directly to you.

---

## 3. The Command Center (4 Mins)
* **Demo**: Click the **Command Center** tab.
* Look at the top profile switcher. Switch profiles from **Owner** to **Transaction Coordinator**.
* Notice how the panels adapt:
  * **Owner Dashboard**: Focuses on high-risk issues, weekly cash-flow projections, and team workload summaries.
  * **TC Dashboard**: Displays critical transaction intake gaps, missing closing dates, and outstanding files.
  * **Marketing Dashboard**: Prioritizes design collateral requests and agent submissions.

---

## 4. The Work Queue & Detail Drawer (5 Mins)
* **Demo**: Click the **Work Queue** tab.
* This is your central inbox. Every item is a task that requires coordinator attention.
* Click on a task to slide open the **Detail Drawer**:
  * **SLA & Audit Log**: Shows exactly when the task was created and its priority status.
  * **Operator Comments**: Add notes or assign the task to another coordinator.
* Try checking multiple boxes and use the bottom **Bulk Actions** bar to reassign or close tasks in one step.

---

## 5. Marketing Request Desk (3 Mins)
* **Demo**: Click the **Marketing Desk** tab.
* Selecting an asset template (e.g. Just Listed Flyer) opens a request form.
* If a coordinator or agent submits a request with missing client details, shapework. automatically detects the gap and creates a correction task in the Work Queue.
* No details are lost, and coordinators do not have to chase down agents via email.

---

## 6. Pipeline / Closing Tracker & Compliance Guards (4 Mins)
* **Demo**: Click the **Closing Tracker** tab.
* The tracker maps active transaction stages and expected closing timelines.
* Review the **Forecast Projections** sub-tab:
  * Displays anticipated commission cash-flow.
  * Adjusts expected numbers based on file confidence ratings (e.g., discounting files with missing signatures).
* Look at the **Intake & Compliance Guards**:
  * Highlights files closing soon that are missing required escrow disclosures.

---

## 7. Approval Center & Weekly Owner Brief (4 Mins)
* **Demo**: Click the **Approvals** tab.
* No outbound emails or texts can be sent to agents or attorneys without human review.
* If shapework. drafts a document request, it is locked in the **Approval Center**.
* As an Owner or Operations Lead, you can edit the text of the draft and click **Approve & Dispatch** to send it.
* **Weekly Owner Brief**: Click the brief tab to copy a clean summary of team accomplishments, cash-flow projections, and owner interruptions avoided this week.

---

## 8. Daily Team Playbook & Launch Next Steps (2 Mins)
### Daily Checklist
1. **Operations Lead**: Check the Pilot Checklist, verify inventory stock, and triage unassigned queue items.
2. **Transaction Coordinator**: Check for missing closing dates and draft document reminder updates.
3. **Marketing Coordinator**: Process new agent requests.
4. **Owner**: Review the weekly brief and authorize queued outbox dispatches.

### After Launch
* As your team gets comfortable managing files manually, we will configure live webhook signals to feed shapework. automatically.
