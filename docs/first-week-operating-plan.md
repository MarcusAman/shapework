# First Week Operating Plan

This guide outlines the step-by-step operating runbook for the brokerage pilot team during their first week running **shapework.**.

---

## Day 0 — Workspace Setup & Onboarding
* **Goal**: Establish the operating record, configure roles, import roster/transaction records, and verify the approval gating loop.
* **Tasks**:
  1. Open Settings -> Customer Onboarding Wizard and activate workspace name: `First Brokerage Pilot Rehearsal`.
  2. Mapped Owner, Operations Lead, TC, and Marketing Coordinator roles.
  3. Load Organization Data Controls, download the 6 Customer Import Templates, paste local data, and import.
  4. Access the Readiness Scorecard to confirm all 10 checklist targets pass.
  5. Export the first Onboarding Playbook Launch Pack for the team.

---

## Day 1 — Launch & Triage Start
* **Goal**: Enable the operators (Owner, Operations Lead, TC, Marketing) to begin monitoring the live Work Queue.
* **Tasks**:
  1. **Owner**: Review the Morning Briefing metrics cards. Verify that all outbound actions require manual sign-off.
  2. **Operations Lead**: Open the Work Queue and assign incoming intake cards.
  3. **Transaction Coordinator**: Filter Closing Tracker for files missing target closing dates. Complete dates in the detail drawer.
  4. **Marketing Coordinator**: Triage flyer requests and deflect manual emails by pointing agents to Canva.

---

## Days 2-3 — Daily Operations & Stabilization
* **Goal**: Establish operational calm. Maintain task hygiene and monitor role routing rules.
* **Tasks**:
  1. Monitor Work Queue for aging items (>24 hours unresolved).
  2. Audit data import warning flags (e.g. transactions missing commission fields).
  3. Ensure that all email drafts sent to agents are successfully locked in the Approval Center before dispatch.
  4. Check Office Readiness yard sign checkout logs to ensure low-stock alarms route only to the Operations Lead.

---

## Days 4-5 — Weekly Measure & Briefing Export
* **Goal**: Quantify avoided administrative hours and outline optimizations.
* **Tasks**:
  1. Navigate to the Weekly Owner Brief.
  2. Confirm total "Avoided Interruptions" count and "Admin Hours Saved".
  3. Export the Weekly Brief markdown report for the executive team.
  4. Open the Launch Decision Console and verify that UAT status is fully cleared.
  5. Schedule the next sprint candidates for webhook integrations.
