About

The practice

We started shapework. because somebody needed to.

Most operational problems in growing businesses are not technology problems. They are design problems. The way work moves through a company is treated as something that just happens. It accumulates. Nobody designs it. Nobody owns it. Nobody is responsible for it.

shapework. is a workflow design practice. We modernize how growing businesses operate. We map what is actually happening, redesign the parts that cost the most, build against the new shape, and stay close until the change holds.

We are deliberately small. We take on a limited number of engagements at a time so the work stays sharp. We are not trying to be the biggest practice in the country. We are trying to be the one that does the work other firms are not willing to do.

Tools amplify whatever shape they are built on top of. Most consultancies sell you the tool. We start with the shape.

Three operators. Three perspectives. One method.

The team

Co-founder

Adam LeMire

Adam's background spans fifteen years of growth and brand strategy work alongside founders and executive teams during scale phases, much of it as a fractional CMO and strategic advisor to real estate brokerages across the country. He's helped owner-led firms build the marketing and operational infrastructure that lets a scaling business punch above its size, without carrying a full executive team to do it.

He served as the strategic growth and brand lead at Bentley's Real Estate during its expansion from approximately $100M to $325M+ in annual sales, where he managed six-figure marketing budgets and architected the positioning that became a primary driver of inbound growth while the team tripled in size. Before that, he spent thirteen years at Thomson Reuters as Head of Audio Technology and Operations, managing distributed teams producing 1,500+ corporate webcasts quarterly and overseeing vendor operations generating 30,000+ financial transcripts annually. He led the transition from in-office workflows to fully automated, remote production systems, the same operational instinct that drives his work today.

He has written for the Wilmington Business Journal on AI and its practical use in modern business. At shapework., he brings the strategy, brand, and AI integration lens, identifying where the leverage lives and shaping how the work shows up in the world.

Berklee College of Music. Lives in Wilmington.
Co-founder

Matt Orr

Matt is the operator half of the partnership. He co-founded Modoc Research Services in 2008, a contract research organization, and grew it into an established firm with national client relationships before executing a successful management buyout and exit in 2018. As Executive Vice President, he ran the operational and financial side of the business, scaling revenue by 300% year over year in its first three years and growing the internal and remote contractor team to over 45 personnel.

Following his exit, Matt founded a business consultancy firm providing principal-level process analysis, organizational restructuring, and fractional C-suite leadership to scaling startups and small businesses. His hands-on operational consulting includes deep-dive audits of IT and financial operations for statewide firms, overseeing key executive placements, and leading critical evaluations of proprietary customer management and data software platforms.

He has lived in Wilmington for over twenty years and works as a real estate advisor at Nest Realty alongside his work with shapework. His background spans research operations, finance, contract negotiation, and small business systems. He brings the structural discipline, risk analysis, and operational mechanics that make the work scalable.

Master's in International Business, Hochschule Bremen. UNCW alum.
Co-founder

Marcus Aman

Marcus is a fixed-operations leader who spent his career inside the kind of high-volume, margin-sensitive operations where inefficiency shows up in the numbers every single day. He led fixed operations at Hendrick Automotive Group, one of the largest privately held automotive retail organizations in the country, running service and repair operations at the scale where small process failures compound into real money. Fourteen-plus years of breaking records in that environment taught him where work actually stalls and what it costs.

He left to build. Marcus founded ShyftAuto out of the inefficiencies he watched frustrate customers and drain shops from the inside, and went on to launch operationally focused ventures including Bayley SmartLot and SmartShop. The through-line is consistent: he sees a broken operational process, names the cost, and builds the system that removes it. That instinct is exactly the work shapework. does.

Based in Wilmington, Marcus brings the frontline operations lens to the partnership, the view from inside a running operation where the work has to get done today. He completed entrepreneurship studies through Wharton Online and holds a degree from the University of Mount Olive. He brings the operational mechanics, the customer-facing discipline, and the builder's bias toward systems that actually hold.

Entrepreneurship, Wharton Online. University of Mount Olive. Lives in Wilmington.

The combination works because Matt structures the systems, Adam identifies the opportunities, and Marcus knows how the work runs on the ground. Together, we shape the work.
